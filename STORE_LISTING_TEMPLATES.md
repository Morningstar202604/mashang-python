## 💬 FAQ for Store Listing

**Q: Does it really work offline?**
A: Yes! The entire CPython 3.13 interpreter is embedded in the app. You can write, run, and debug code without any internet connection.

**Q: How is this different from Pydroid or other IDEs?**
A: We're a learning platform, not just an editor. Each lesson includes guided tutorials, auto-graded exercises, and a gamified progression system. Our goal is to teach you, not just provide a blank canvas.

**Q: Is it really free?**
A: Yes! All 30 lessons are completely free with no ads. We offer an optional Pro subscription for advanced features (turtle graphics, AI tutor), but the core experience is 100% free forever.

**Q: What Android versions are supported?**
A: Android 7.0+ (API 24). We support both arm64-v8a and x86_64 architectures.

**Q: Can teachers use this in classrooms?**
A: Absolutely! The MIT license allows commercial and educational use. Many teachers recommend it to students for homework practice.

**Q: Will there be an iOS version?**
A: Currently we only support Android due to our tech stack (Chaquopy). iOS is on our long-term roadmap.

**Q: How much storage does it need?**
A: The APK is ~35-40MB. After installation and caching, expect ~100MB total usage.

**Q: Can I contribute new lessons?**
A: Yes! We're open source on GitHub. Fork the repo, add your content, and submit a PR. We welcome contributions!
